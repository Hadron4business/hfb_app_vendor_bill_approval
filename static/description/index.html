Vendor Bill Approval

Vendor bills go to the right people automatically. They describe the expense and approve it - only approved bills can be posted.

The problem

A vendor bill arrives and the accountant does not know what it is for, who ordered it or whether the goods or services were delivered. The answer is chased by e-mail or on paper, and bills get posted without anyone responsible having seen them.

What you get

Rules, not manual routing

Rules by amount (taxes included) and vendor decide who approves: e.g. every bill to the office manager, IT vendors to the IT manager, from 10,000 also to the CFO.

No Accounting access needed

Approvers work in their own Bill Approvals menu: vendor, amounts, lines and the PDF of the bill side by side. Department heads do not need access to Accounting.

Expense description

Approvers write what the expense is for (required per rule). The description is kept on the bill and in its chatter.

Approve, reject, hand over

Reject with a reason (the accountant is notified), or hand the approval over to a colleague during holidays - with history.

Posting blocked

A bill cannot be posted while an approval is waiting or rejected. The accountant gets a message when everything is approved.

Status everywhere

Approval status on the bill (ribbon, Approvals tab) and as a column in the list of bills.

How to use it

1
Go to Invoicing > Configuration > Settings, section Vendor Bills, enable Vendor Bill Approval (and optionally automatic requests for new bills).
2
Create your rules in Invoicing > Configuration > Bill Approval Rules: amount from, vendors (empty = all), approvers, description required.
3
Create or import a vendor bill. Approval is requested automatically (or with Request Approval). The approvers get an activity.
4
The approver opens Bill Approvals > To Approve, checks the PDF, describes the expense and clicks Approve - or Reject / Hand Over.
5
When all approvals are done, the accountant is notified and posts the bill. On the bill, the Approvals tab shows who approved what and when.

Good to know

  • Approvers named in a rule automatically get the "Vendor Bill Approver" access; they only see their own approvals.
  • Use "Ask Someone Else" on the Approvals tab to add an approver to one bill only.
  • A rejected bill can be sent for approval again with Request Approval after it has been corrected.
  • Applies to vendor bills and vendor refunds; customer invoices are not affected.
